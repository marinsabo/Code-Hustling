We bring out the light in you

The community has grown a lot lately — more than 100,000 people follow what we do. Our goal is simple: push you to become the best version of yourself in this career. Every developer has a spark; we're here to help light it.

If you're new to development, it's hard to stay motivated. It can feel like you're getting nowhere, like you've wasted your time and money, or that nobody will ever notice what you can do. Don't give up. We're here with the daily dose of motivation and discipline.
